## Bounded research gap

The reviewed studies cover drone classification and localization, target-conditioned spatial processing, phase-aware localization, and interpretable or adaptive evidence weighting. Within this reviewed set, no study was identified that combines decision-relevant acoustic evidence with a matching multichannel reference path, explicit physical-source compatibility states, observability-aware unavailability, and direct control of reliance on the original classifier decision. This is a bounded literature finding, not a universal novelty claim.
